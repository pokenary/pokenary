Pokenary — Complete Pokédex Generations 1 to 9

Pokenary is a free, fast and beautifully designed Pokédex web app covering all 1025 Pokémon from Generation 1 through Generation 9, including Scarlet and Violet, Sword and Shield, Sun and Moon, and all previous games.

Features

  • Full Pokédex with all 1025 Pokémon from Gen 1 to Gen 9
  • Base stats, stat radar chart, min and max values at level 100
  • Normal and shiny sprite comparison for every Pokémon
  • Mega Evolutions including Mega Charizard X and Y, Mega Mewtwo X and Y
  • All regional variants: Alolan forms, Galarian forms, Hisuian forms, Paldean forms
  • Complete evolution chains with clickable navigation
  • Type effectiveness chart showing weaknesses and resistances
  • Filter by Generation, Type, and Mega Evolution availability
  • Search by name or Pokédex number
  • Mobile-friendly design optimized for iPhone and Android
